Your treatment journey

A clear, step‑by‑step process from first contact to full recovery.

1. Consultation & diagnosis

Detailed history, clinical examination and review of X‑rays/MRI to understand the exact cause of your problem.

2. Personalised treatment plan

Non‑surgical options like medicines, injections and physiotherapy are explored first wherever appropriate.

3. Surgery when needed

When surgery is the best choice, advanced arthroscopy and joint replacement techniques are used for better outcomes.

4. Rehabilitation & follow‑up

Guided exercises, regular review and long‑term follow‑up to help you safely return to work, sport and daily life.

Dr Akshay Jathkar MBBS; MS Orthopedics (Sion Hospital, Mumbai); Fellowship in Arthroscopy (Hungary)

Dr Akshay Jathkar is an orthopaedic surgeon with over 8 years of experience in treating bone, joint and muscle conditions. His interests include sports injuries, arthroscopic procedures and joint replacement surgery.

His approach focuses on precise diagnosis, minimally invasive techniques wherever possible, and structured rehabilitation so that patients can safely return to work, family life and sports.
