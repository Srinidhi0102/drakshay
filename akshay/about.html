About Dr Akshay Jathkar & the clinic

Dedicated orthopaedic care for fractures, joint problems, spine issues and sports injuries, with a focus on fast recovery and long‑term function.

Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon

Dr Akshay Jathkar

MBBS; MS Orthopedics (Sion Hospital, Mumbai); Fellowship in Arthroscopy (Hungary)

Dr Akshay Jathkar is a board‑certified orthopaedic surgeon with over 8 years of experience in treating bone, joint, and muscle conditions. He specialises in sports medicine, joint replacement, and minimally invasive arthroscopic procedures aimed at faster recovery and long‑term joint preservation.

Orthopaedic focus areas

A complete range of orthopaedic services from first consultation to surgery, rehabilitation and long‑term follow‑up.

Fractures & trauma

Immediate and follow‑up care for simple and complex fractures.

  • Upper and lower limb fractures
  • Operative and non‑operative management
  • Post‑fracture rehabilitation guidance

Joint & arthritis care

Treatment for knee, hip, shoulder and other joint problems.

  • Pain and mobility assessment
  • Joint injections where indicated
  • Planning for joint replacement when needed

Spine & back pain

Evaluation and management of neck, back and radiating leg pain.

  • Non‑surgical spine care
  • Guided physiotherapy plans
  • Referral for advanced spine surgery if required

Care philosophy

Individualised treatment

No two patients or injuries are exactly the same. Treatment plans are customised to age, activity level and expectations.

Transparent communication

Reports and X‑rays are explained in simple language so you clearly understand your condition and options.

Team approach

When needed, physiotherapists and other specialists are involved to provide complete musculoskeletal care.

Long‑term results

The goal is to help you return to work, sport and daily life with confidence and minimal restriction.
