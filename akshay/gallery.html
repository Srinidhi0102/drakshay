Dr Akshay Jathkar MBBS; MS Orthopedics (Sion Hospital, Mumbai); Fellowship in Arthroscopy (Hungary)

Dr Akshay Jathkar is an orthopaedic surgeon with over 8 years of experience in treating bone, joint and muscle conditions. His interests include sports injuries, arthroscopic procedures and joint replacement surgery.

His approach focuses on precise diagnosis, minimally invasive techniques wherever possible, and structured rehabilitation so that patients can safely return to work, family life and sports.
